What we do

Seven services, each with a clear deliverable

Pick one or combine them. Every engagement ends with a written report and a prioritised fix list your team can act on.

🛡

EDR Consulting & Deployment

Choose, design and configure endpoint protection. We handle full deployment for ESET and Kaspersky.

  • Product selection and sizing
  • Policy and exclusion tuning
  • Rollout to endpoints and servers
  • Handover and admin training
📈

Wazuh SIEM Deployment & Integration

An open-source SIEM and XDR platform deployed and connected to your environment for central monitoring and alerting.

  • Architecture and server deployment
  • Agent rollout on endpoints and servers
  • Firewall, EDR and Active Directory integration
  • Alert rules and dashboards
🔥

Firewall Audit

Configuration review of FortiGate and Sophos firewalls against best practice.

  • Rule base and unused rules
  • VPN and admin access
  • Firmware and hardening
  • Logging and segmentation
✉

Email Security

Protect your mail from phishing, spoofing and malware, and fix the settings that let them through.

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC setup
  • Anti-phishing and anti-spam policies
  • Mail gateway and filtering configuration
  • Mail platform hardening
🕷

Web Application Penetration Test

Manual testing of your application the way a real attacker would approach it.

  • OWASP Top 10 coverage
  • Authentication and access control
  • Business logic flaws
  • Retest after fixes
🖥

Server Security Audit

Find misconfigurations and security gaps on Windows and Linux servers.

  • Hardening baseline check
  • Patch and service exposure
  • Accounts and permissions
  • Backup and logging review

Quick Vulnerability Assessment

A fast external check of your domain and exposed systems, at no cost and no obligation.

  • Domain and DNS review
  • Exposed services and ports
  • Summary of key findings
